Email [putri@lunagraph.com](mailto:putri@lunagraph.com) for help with your account, subscription, invoices, or a billing question before you upgrade.

You do not need a Lunagraph account to ask a question. If you already have an account, include the workspace name and the email address you use for Lunagraph.

To help us understand the issue, include:

- What you were trying to do and what happened.
- Any error text, or a screenshot with sensitive information removed.

Don't send passwords, payment card details, API keys, or other secrets.

For plan questions, start with [Free and paid plans](/help-center/account-billing/plans). For setup and product walkthroughs, use the [getting started tutorial](/tutorial).
