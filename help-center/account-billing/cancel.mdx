title: "Cancel a paid plan"
description: "Schedule a move to Free at the end of your paid period."
icon: "calendar"
---

Your paid plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, then moves to Free. Only the workspace owner can cancel.

## Schedule cancellation

<Frame caption="On a paid workspace, choose Downgrade to Free on the Free plan card.">
  <img src="/images/help-center/billing-downgrade-to-free.jpg" alt="The Free plan card showing the Downgrade to Free button for a paid workspace." />
</Frame>

1. Select your paid workspace and open **Settings → Billing**.
2. On the **Free** plan card, choose **Downgrade to Free**.
3. Complete the cancellation flow in your browser.
4. Return to **Billing** for the same workspace to check the result.

A scheduled cancellation shows **Canceled** in the summary and **Moves to Free at period end** on the Free card. When available, the summary shows **Active until [date], then moves to Free**.

<Frame caption="Example of a scheduled cancellation. The paid plan remains active until the displayed end date.">
  <img src="/images/help-center/billing-cancellation-scheduled.jpg" alt="Billing summary showing Canceled, the date the workspace moves to Free, and Don't cancel." />
</Frame>

A Free workspace without a subscription has nothing to cancel and doesn't show **Downgrade to Free**.

## Keep your paid plan

Before the paid period ends, the owner can choose **Don't cancel** in the summary. Check that the pending cancellation has been removed.

If the flow fails or the summary doesn't update, [contact support](/help-center/contact-support).

Canceling Lunagraph doesn't cancel subscriptions with an external agent provider. Manage those with the provider.
