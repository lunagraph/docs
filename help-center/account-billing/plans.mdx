If you cancel a paid plan, it stays active until the end of the period you paid for, then moves to Free.

## Open your workspace's billing settings

Select your workspace and open **Settings → Billing**. Only the workspace owner can change plans or manage payments.

## What you see on Free

<Frame caption="Example Free workspace.">
  <img src="/images/help-center/billing-free.jpg" alt="Lunagraph Billing and plans on Free, with the current-plan summary and upgrade choices." />
</Frame>

The summary shows **Free** and explains cancellation before you upgrade. Owners can choose **Monthly** or **Annual** and select a paid plan.

A Free workspace with no subscription has no **Invoices and payment** or **Downgrade to Free** button.

## What you see on a paid plan

<Frame caption="Example paid workspace with two editor seats. Review checkout or invoices for actual charges.">
  <img src="/images/help-center/billing-paid-two-editors.jpg" alt="Lunagraph Billing and plans with two paid editor seats, Invoices and payment, and Downgrade to Free." />
</Frame>

The summary shows your plan price and seat count, plus a renewal date when available. Paid plans are billed per editor seat; viewers are free. Review checkout or your invoice for actual charges.

Workspace owners can:

- Open **Invoices and payment** to launch the [Stripe customer portal](https://docs.stripe.com/customer-management) in your browser. There you can view and download invoices, update your payment method, or schedule cancellation of your paid subscription.
- Choose **Downgrade to Free** on the Free card to [cancel the paid plan](/help-center/account-billing/cancel).

If you aren't the owner, the summary identifies who manages billing. If your subscription has fully ended and you need an older invoice, [contact support](/help-center/contact-support).

## Your agent provider is separate

Your Lunagraph plan is separate from an external agent provider's subscription or API billing. Canceling Lunagraph doesn't cancel those subscriptions.
