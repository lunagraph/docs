Only the workspace owner can upgrade. If you later cancel, your paid plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, then moves to Free.

## Choose a plan

<Frame caption="Example Free workspace.">
  <img src="/images/help-center/billing-free.jpg" alt="Lunagraph Billing and plans on Free, with the current-plan summary and upgrade choices." />
</Frame>

1. Select your workspace and open **Settings → Billing**.
2. Review the plan cards and choose **Monthly** or **Annual**.
3. Choose **Upgrade to** on the plan you want.
4. In your browser, review the plan, seats, amount, and billing interval before confirming payment.

Existing paid subscribers may see Stripe's billing portal instead of a new checkout.

## After payment

Return to **Billing** for the same workspace and check the current-plan summary.

If checkout doesn't open, your plan doesn't update, or you have a question about a charge, [contact support](/help-center/contact-support). Include the workspace name and any error text, without payment card details.

See [Free and paid plans](/help-center/account-billing/plans) for the controls available before and after upgrading.
