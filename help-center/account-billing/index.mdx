Your Lunagraph plan belongs to your workspace. Open **Settings → Billing** to see **Billing & plans** for the selected workspace.

If you're deciding whether to upgrade, start with [Free and paid plans](/help-center/account-billing/plans). You can cancel a paid plan from billing settings; it stays active until the end of the period you paid for, then moves to Free.

Only the workspace owner can change the plan or manage payments. If you need help, [contact support](/help-center/contact-support).
