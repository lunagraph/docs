Only the workspace owner can access **Invoices and payment**. The button appears when the workspace has a subscription.

## Open invoices and payment

<Frame caption="Example paid workspace: Invoices and payment appears in the current-plan summary.">
  <img src="/images/help-center/billing-invoices-payment.jpg" alt="Billing summary with the Invoices and payment button at the top right." />
</Frame>

1. Select your workspace and open **Settings → Billing**.
2. Choose **Invoices and payment** in the current-plan summary.
3. The **Stripe customer portal** opens in your browser.

In Stripe, you can:

- Change your paid plan using **Update plan**, when available.
- [Cancel your paid plan](/help-center/account-billing/cancel) at the end of the period you paid for.
- View and download your invoices.
- Update the payment method for your subscription.

Read [Stripe's customer portal documentation](https://docs.stripe.com/customer-management) for more about the portal.

## Why the button may be missing

- **Free with no subscription:** the button isn't shown.
- **You aren't the owner:** ask the owner shown in the summary.
- **Your subscription has fully ended:** [contact support](/help-center/contact-support) for older invoices. You don't need to upgrade again to ask for help.

Check that you've selected the correct workspace.

## If a payment fails

If the summary shows **Payment failed**, ask the owner to update the payment method through **Invoices and payment**.

If the portal doesn't open or a charge is unclear, [contact support](/help-center/contact-support). Never send card numbers, security codes, or passwords.
